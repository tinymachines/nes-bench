**State: open.** Sitting 1 holds. Step 1.1 holds, from the connector's

Nothing in this sitting is soldered and nothing is built. It is a meter,
two probes and a game.

## What the tools already know

Checked from the workstation before this was written, so that a probe
setting could not be mistaken for a finding:

- The scope answers, and its setup is saved and restored around every
  measurement, because it belongs to another experiment.
- **The console appears to be off.** CH3, the video probe, read
  1.156 Vpp with a 3.571 MHz colour burst this morning and now reads
  0.032 Vpp. CH1 and CH2 are flat at zero too. That is what a
  powered-down console looks like from here.
- CH1 and CH2 read the same at 1x and at 10x, so the probes are not
  merely mis-attenuated. They are not seeing a live line.

## The colour map, for use at the bench

From the port housing's own moulded numbers (`docs/lab/01-port-housing-*`):

| pin | signal | wire |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | GND | brown |
| 2 | CLK | red |
| 3 | OUT0, the latch | orange |
| 4 | D0, the data | yellow |
| 5 | D3 | white |
| 6 | D4 | blue |
| 7 | +5 V | purple |

This is the housing telling you its own numbering. It does not prove the
harness reaches the board header unswapped, and it does not prove the
published function table is right for this board. Step 1.2 is what tests
both, and the test is that **purple reads about 5 V**.

## Step A, you: power it up

Console on, a game in it that polls the pad, and an original pad in the
port you are probing. Confirm the picture is on the screen.

## Step B, you: four readings with the meter

Black lead on **pin 1, brown**. Nothing plugged into the port you are
measuring.

| read | expect |
|---|---|
| pin 7, purple | about 5 V |
| pin 3, orange | high, idle |
| pin 2, red | high, idle |
| pin 4, yellow | high, pulled up |

Report the four numbers. **If purple is not about 5 V, stop.** Either
the map is wrong or the console's supply is, and the tool will refuse to
go further rather than let anything be built onto it.

## Step D, you: two probes

- **CH1** on **pin 3, orange**, OUT0.
- **CH2** on **pin 2, red**, CLK.
- Both grounds on **pin 1, brown**.
- Probes at **1x** if they have a switch.
- Pad in that port, game polling.

## Step E, the tools: step 2.1

This is the one that matters. It arms both channels, waits for a latch
pulse, reads twelve million points and measures four things:

- the latch pulse width,
- the clock pulse width,
- the gap between clocks,
- the polls per second.

Those four are marked **authored** across `docs/wiring.md` and the
timing sheet, and have been since before any of this was built. This
step retires them.
