# The menu's poll in its first two seconds: the model polls at PPU line
# 119.83 for latches 0..126 and at 119.36 from 127 on
# (mario-dissection.md, "Done on the part"); the part had only been
# recorded past latch 290. Three channels: the bridge's TRIG on CH1
# (armed BEFORE the reset, since RESET clears the bridge's trigger and
# the scope takes seconds to arm), the latch on CH2, the video on CH3;
# 6 M points is the DS1054Z's depth with three channels. TRIG 8
# marks latch 8 on the record so poll-line.py numbers the rest.
POWER ON
WAIT 2 S
MODE INJECT
SET 00
ARM early8 1,2,3 0.2 -0.5 CH1 0.005 6000000
RESET
TRIG 8
WAIT 48
CAPTURE
POWER OFF
